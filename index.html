01WHY PULSE POINT?

Confidence before
the emergency.

Emergencies can happen anywhere. Knowing the first safe step can help someone stay calm while professional help is on the way.

Pulse Point is a student-led initiative to make first-aid education easier to access, practise, and remember across the KCA University community.
